Privacy policy

Apple Mail MCP · last updated 25 October 2026

Apple Mail MCP is a personal tool used by its developer, Louis De Beule, on his own Mac and with his own Gmail account only. It is not offered to other people.

What it accesses in Gmail

With the Gmail permission its owner grants, the tool only:

  • finds one message by its Message-ID and reads that message's labels, and
  • adds or removes the Inbox label and a label the owner chose (such as "Advertisements") on that message, when the owner's own mail sorting asks for it, and undoes that on request.

It does not read message content through Gmail's API, and it does not send, delete or change any other mail.

Where data is kept

The Gmail sign-in is kept in the macOS Keychain on the owner's Mac. Message identifiers and labels are used in memory to make one change and are not stored by the tool. No Gmail data is sent to the developer, sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising or to train AI models.

Google API Services

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which the tool can no longer reach Gmail.
